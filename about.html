About Harbor City

Harbor City Church is a neighborhood church learning to follow Jesus together with honesty, hospitality, and hope.

Our heartbeat

We want to be a church where people can belong before they have everything figured out, discover the way of Jesus, and serve their neighbors.

Our story

Harbor City began as a small gathering of friends who wanted to create a welcoming Christian community in Grand Rapids.
